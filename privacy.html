Email Manager - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-12

Email Manager is a personal tool operated by its developer for their own Gmail account only.

Data accessed

With the developer's permission, the app uses the Gmail scopes gmail.modify and gmail.insert to read the sender and metadata of recent unread messages, apply labels, mark messages as read, and move messages to trash or the "Unwanted" label.

Data storage and sharing

Email content is processed in memory during each run and is not stored by the app. The app keeps only a list of unwanted sender addresses and OAuth credentials, held as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets. No data is sold, shared with, or transferred to third parties, and none is used for advertising or to train AI models.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
